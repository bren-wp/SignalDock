# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

SignalDock welcomes technical discussion, bug reports and contributions from people who engage constructively and respectfully.

Expected behavior includes:

- focusing criticism on code, design and evidence rather than people;
- providing enough technical context for others to reproduce or evaluate a claim;
- respecting privacy and never publishing another person's sensitive log data without authorization;
- accepting that maintainers may reject changes that conflict with SignalDock's local-first, security or data-integrity principles;
- avoiding harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, spam and intentionally disruptive behavior.

## Scope

This applies to SignalDock repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions and other project-managed spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove content and may restrict participation when behavior materially disrupts the project or violates these standards. Serious security or privacy concerns should be reported privately according to `SECURITY.md`.
